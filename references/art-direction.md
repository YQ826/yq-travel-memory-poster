# Art direction

## Core language

Record a place through selective memory rather than realism. Combine modern flat composition with tactile analog material: large matte gouache and cut-paper shapes, fine uniform paper tooth, awkward handmade contours, restrained accents, and quiet negative space.

## Background and ink

- Card paper: very pale warm ivory, approximately `#F7F2E8`; bright and airy, never pure white, tan, brown, gray, or strongly yellowed.
- Structural ink: deep muted navy, approximately `#123A63`.
- Derive 5–8 matte color families from the photo. Favor dusty midtones with one or two crisp source-derived accents.
- Use flat colored shadows. Do not use gradients.

## Shapes and texture

- Make the main illustration read first as 3–6 oversized masses.
- Reduce buildings, vehicles, terrain, water, sky, and crowds to decisive chunky planes.
- Compress foliage into 1–3 lumpy tonal clusters; reduce people to compact faceless silhouettes.
- Use opaque gouache, cut-paper, risograph, or screen-print-like fills with chalky edges, subtle density variation, and light misregistration.
- Apply one fine monochromatic paper tooth consistently across illustration, stickers, and footer paper pieces.

## Scene-specific simplification

- Sky: 2–3 broad flat paper layers, not streaky painted clouds.
- Water: 3–4 wide irregular horizontal bands, not small highlights or repeated dashes.
- Architecture: large facade planes, window groups, and silhouette-defining edges only.
- Vegetation: grouped canopy masses and a few silhouette-defining trunks or fronds only.
- Vehicles: preserve recognizable proportion and signature color while suppressing mechanical detail.

## Typography

- Large relaxed hand-painted script for the title.
- Small condensed uppercase navy text for subtitle, sticker labels, and paper ephemera.
- Use one short, scene-specific subtitle. Keep all generated English concise enough to remain legible.

## Avoid

- photographic patches or photo filters inside the card;
- visible marker, acrylic, oil, or watercolor strokes;
- wet blends, blooms, washes, soft gradients, dense short dashes, sparkle, or detailed leaves;
- glossy 3D, smooth polished vector art, anime, clip art, dramatic lighting, or clutter;
- unintended readable signage, signatures, watermarks, dates, prices, or advertising.
