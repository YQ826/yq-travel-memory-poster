# Layout specification

## Final poster

- Default canvas: `1024×1536` PNG, vertical 2:3.
- Split: `y=768`; photo and card are each exactly `1024×768`.
- The top photo fills its half without a frame, gutter, caption, or color adjustment.
- The bottom card fills its half edge-to-edge. Keep every outer border segment inside the canvas.

## Bottom card

Use one horizontal 4:3 card on very pale warm-ivory uncoated paper.

- Continuous thin dark-navy rounded outer border; the full top edge and both top corners must be visible.
- Main area above the footer:
  - left approximately 62%: city/scene title, short uppercase subtitle, and one large source-derived illustration;
  - right approximately 38%: `TRAVEL STICKERS` and exactly six die-cut stickers in a clear 2×3 arrangement.
- Footer: full width, separated by a thin navy horizontal rule, divided into exactly four equal cells.
- Use thin navy internal rules throughout. Avoid heavy frames, shadows, or nested cards.

## Six stickers

Choose six distinct visible motifs with useful variety:

1. primary subject or landmark fragment;
2. secondary subject or grouped variation;
3. environmental form;
4. structural fragment;
5. functional object;
6. small atmospheric or scale cue.

Each sticker has a warm-white irregular die-cut edge, a restrained flat shadow, and a short uppercase English label. Remove incidental advertising, prices, watermarks, and unreadable source text.

## Four footer keepsakes

The footer records travel paper ephemera, not four more object stickers. Use these fixed artifact types from left to right:

1. **Place postmark** — small round postal mark with place text and short cancellation lines.
2. **Travel ticket** — small notched ticket stub with a scene-specific route or activity phrase and a tiny decorative serial.
3. **Environment stamp** — small upright perforated postage stamp containing only abstract scene-derived color bands, shapes, or texture fields.
4. **Memory note** — small torn-edge paper note with one concise two-line handwritten English memory and a tiny tape strip.

Rules:

- Keep each paper item at roughly 35–50% of its cell height or width and surround it with generous empty paper.
- Allow only a subtle 1–2 degree handmade rotation.
- Put text inside the paper artifact; add no category caption below it.
- Do not repeat any of the six sticker motifs inside the footer stamp or note.
- Do not use cameras, map pins, hearts, emoji, generic transport icons, analytical charts, waveforms, route arrows, or the word `CONNECTION`.
